Collaboration

We welcome academic and industry collaborations in the production of drug-target proteins, particularly GPCRs and membrane proteins, their structural and functional analysis, the discovery of function-controlling molecules (antibodies, AI-enabled de novo binders and DEL-derived small molecules), and structure-guided drug discovery.

Students & Fellows

We actively welcome master's and doctoral students, doctoral students in the working-professional program, and JSPS Research Fellows (PD/DC). If you would like to join the group, please contact us by email.
