Protein Science Group · Kansai Medical University

Mastering proteins. Pioneering new medicines.Producing high-quality proteins. Solving their structures.
Creating molecules that control them.

Protein science for drug discovery.

Our technical strength is the production of high-quality preparations of GPCRs and other membrane proteins. Starting from these preparations, we determine structures by cryo-EM and discover molecules that control the target — antibodies, monobodies/nanobodies, AI-designed de novo binders and small molecules from DNA-encoded library (DEL) screening. Our goal is to turn them into diverse modalities that control protein function and develop them into new medicines.

    Research focus

    From high-quality proteins to function-controlling molecules and new medicines

    Protein production, structure determination and molecular discovery form one continuous workflow toward new medicines.

    Protein Production→Structure→Molecular Discovery→Drug Development

    Step 01 · Protein production

    High-Quality Protein Preparations

    For each target we build an expression system in insect or mammalian cells, solubilize and purify the membrane protein, assemble its complexes, and confirm homogeneity, stability and activity before anything else begins.

    Step 02 · Structure

    Structures & Molecular Mechanisms

    Using cryo-EM as the central method, we capture distinct functional states of GPCRs and other membrane proteins and connect them to pharmacological data to explain ligand recognition and signaling selectivity.

    Step 03 · Molecular discovery

    Molecules That Control the Target

    With these preparations in hand, we generate antibodies, monobodies/nanobodies (through collaboration), AI-designed de novo binders and DEL-derived small molecules that selectively control target states and function — then develop them as functional modalities toward new medicines.

    Core expertise & approaches

    Protein science at the core, expanding toward broader drug targets

    We extend our membrane-protein production technology, and the structural analysis and molecular discovery built on it, from GPCRs to a wider range of drug targets.

    Protein production

    High-Quality Protein Preparations

    Expression-system construction in insect and mammalian cells, membrane-protein solubilization, purification and complex formation, and quality control — producing preparations that stand up to structural analysis and screening.

    Targets

    GPCRs & Membrane Proteins

    Major targets include opioid, orexin, prostaglandin E2, muscarinic and bombesin receptors.

    Structure

    Cryo-Electron Microscopy

    High-resolution structural determination of membrane proteins and signaling complexes for drug discovery.

    Function

    Functional & Signaling Analysis

    Quantitative analysis of ligand-dependent function and signaling selectivity, integrated with structural information.

    Molecular discovery

    Antibodies, Monobodies/Nanobodies, de novo Binders & Small Molecules

    Antibody production, AI-enabled de novo binder design and DEL-based small-molecule discovery are carried out in-house; monobodies and nanobodies are obtained through collaboration. Together they give us function-controlling molecules in many formats.

    Drug development

    From Modalities to Medicines

    We refine these molecules by structure-based and AI-enabled design and develop them as functional modalities that control protein function, aiming at new medicines.
