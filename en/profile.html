Profile

My group's technical strength is the production of high-quality preparations of drug-target proteins; on that foundation we pursue structural analysis and the discovery of molecules that control target function. GPCRs are a major focus. For each target we go from expression-system construction through purification, complex formation and quality control, and integrate cryo-electron microscopy, pharmacological functional analysis, studies of ligand subtype selectivity and biased signaling, and molecular discovery with antibodies, monobodies/nanobodies, AI-enabled de novo binders and DEL-derived small molecules. Our goal is to create diverse modalities that control protein function and develop them into new medicines.

Research Interests

  • Production of high-quality membrane-protein preparations (expression, purification, complex formation, quality control)
  • GPCR and membrane-protein structural biology by cryo-electron microscopy
  • Ligand subtype selectivity and biased signaling
  • Molecular discovery: antibodies, monobodies/nanobodies, AI-enabled de novo binders and small molecules by DEL screening
  • Functional modalities and their development into new medicines

Major Research Targets

Major targets include opioid receptors, orexin receptors, prostaglandin E2 receptors, muscarinic receptors and bombesin receptors, together with other GPCRs and membrane proteins studied from both structural and functional perspectives.
